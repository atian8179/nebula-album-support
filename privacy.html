隐私政策 · 生效日期：2026 年 9 月 29 日 / Effective September 29, 2026

1. 照片与设备内处理

星云相册由开发者 Pengtian Feng 提供。应用在你授权后访问照片和必要的拍摄时间、照片内已有地点等元数据，用于本地语义检索、相似照片处理、星迹和照片呈现。照片、搜索词、照片特征向量及本地索引不会发送至开发者服务器或云端模型服务。应用不要求注册账户，不包含广告追踪或第三方行为分析 SDK。

2. 权限与选择

你可以授予完整或有限照片访问权限，并随时在 iOS 设置中更改。有限权限只允许处理已授权照片。照片保存权限用于你主动导出；运动信息只用于设备倾斜交互。应用不获取你的实时定位，地点来自已授权照片的元数据。分享及删除仅在你主动操作后执行；系统可能要求再次确认。

3. 本地保存与删除

应用在设备内保存索引、照片引用、星迹、偏好设置和使用次数。卸载应用会移除其本地数据，不会删除系统照片库原片。系统设备备份可能包含应用数据，其处理由你的 Apple 备份设置决定。撤回照片权限不会删除原片，但会限制相关功能。

4. Apple 服务与购买

照片如储存在 iCloud，系统可能按你的设置下载原片。内购、订阅、付款及恢复购买由 Apple App Store 处理，应用通过 StoreKit 在设备上检查购买状态；开发者不接收银行卡资料。Apple 自身的数据处理受其隐私政策约束。自动续费订阅可在系统订阅设置中取消，终身档位是一次性购买。

5. 联系、第三方网页及政策更新

当你主动通过电子邮件联系开发者时，我们仅使用你提供的邮件地址、问题描述及自愿提供的材料来回复和排查问题，不用于广告或出售。请避免发送不必要的私人照片。你可通过下方邮箱请求删除支持沟通中提供的信息。公开支持网站由 GitHub Pages 托管；访问网页时，托管服务可能处理正常的网络请求日志，见 GitHub 隐私声明。应用内本地照片不会因此上传。政策如有变化，会更新此页和生效日期。

1. On-device photo processing

Nebula Album is provided by developer Pengtian Feng. With your permission, it accesses photos and their existing date and location metadata for on-device semantic search, similar-photo tools, saved memories and visual presentations. Photos, queries, feature vectors and the local index are not sent to developer servers or cloud model services. The app requires no account and includes no advertising tracking or third-party behavioral analytics SDK.

2. Permissions and control

You may grant full or limited photo access and change it in iOS Settings. Limited access restricts processing to authorized photos. Add-photo access is used for exports you initiate; motion information is used only for tilt interactions. The app does not obtain your live location; locations come from authorized photo metadata. Sharing or deleting photos happens only after your action, and may require a system confirmation.

3. Storage and deletion

The app stores its index, photo references, saved memories, settings and usage counters locally. Uninstalling removes app-local data without deleting originals in your system photo library. Device backups may contain app data according to your Apple backup settings. Revoking access limits functionality without deleting originals.

4. Apple services and purchases

The system may download iCloud originals according to your settings. Apple processes purchases, subscriptions, payments and restoration. The app checks entitlements on device through StoreKit; the developer does not receive payment-card information. Apple's processing is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Auto-renewing subscriptions can be canceled in system subscription settings; lifetime access is a one-time purchase.

5. Support and website

If you email us, we use the address, issue description and materials you voluntarily provide only to reply and troubleshoot, not for advertising or sale. Avoid sending unnecessary private photos. Contact us to request deletion of information supplied during support. This public support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose normal request logging is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement. This does not upload your app-local photos. We publish policy changes and a revised effective date on this page.
