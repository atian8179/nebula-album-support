隐私政策

更新及生效日期：2026 年 10 月 7 日 · 适用于 1.0（14）及其本地历史资料 / Updated and effective October 7, 2026

1. 照片与设备内处理

星云相册（Stardrift）由开发者 Pengtian Feng 提供。经你授权，应用访问系统照片库中的照片及其已有的拍摄时间、地点等元数据，用于设备内语义检索、相似照片处理、星迹和照片呈现。照片、搜索词、图像特征向量及索引不会发送至开发者服务器或云端模型服务。无需注册账户；应用没有广告追踪或第三方行为分析 SDK。

2. 人脸数据

以下说明区分“应用在本机处理或保存”和“开发者收集或上传”。本机处理仍涉及人脸资料；不能因为不上传就将其描述为不存在人脸数据。

2.1 数据种类与来源

应用包含设备内人物整理组件，只对已获得照片访问权限的照片进行处理，不调用相机、TrueDepth、ARKit 或 Face ID 获取面部数据。处理时，Apple Vision 在内存中检测人脸区域、面部关键点和清晰度；关键点用于对齐人脸，不单独持久保存。组件可在本机保存 112×112 人脸缩略图、用于相似度匹配的数值特征向量、人脸质量分数、关联照片的本机标识、分组标识、分组平均特征、用户填写的称呼以及手动排除标记。

2.2 用途与启用状态

人脸数据的唯一用途是在用户自己的照片库中归组同一人物、显示人物预览、纠正分组，以及按用户填写的称呼查找照片。应用不会据此确认现实身份，不用于身份认证、广告、追踪、出售、向外部身份库匹配或训练、改进机器学习模型。当前 1.0（14）版本默认关闭人物整理，正式界面不提供开启人物扫描的入口；普通语义检索无需开启此组件。旧内测版本如曾启用，本机可能仍保有此前生成的资料，可按第 2.4 节清除。

2.3 存储、披露与共享

人脸缩略图、特征和人物分组保存在当前设备应用沙盒的本地 SwiftData 数据库中。Vision 和随应用内置的 SFace/Core ML 模型在设备内推理；人脸数据不会发送给开发者、模型提供方、广告商、分析服务或其他第三方，应用也没有人脸数据上传、云端推理或跨设备人物同步功能。系统设备备份可能依据你的 Apple 设置包含该数据库；这由 Apple 的设备备份功能管理，并非应用向模型服务上传。开发者无法访问你的设备备份。

2.4 保留期限、撤回与删除

临时检测区域和关键点只用于处理过程，不单独存入数据库。已持久保存的人脸特征、缩略图、分组和称呼没有按天数自动过期的机制，会保留到你主动清除或删除应用为止。自 1.0（14）起，可进入“我的 → 隐私管理 → 清除人物数据”，二次确认后关闭人物整理，等待正在执行的扫描结束，再删除本机全部人脸记录、缩略图、分组特征和称呼；保留系统原照片、普通照片索引和星迹。新安装且没有人物资料时也可执行此操作。清除失败会明确提示重试，不会提示删除成功。你也可在系统设置中撤回照片权限以停止后续访问；仅撤回权限不等于清除已保存资料。选择系统的“删除 App”会移除当前安装的应用数据，“卸载 App/Offload App”可能保留数据。上述清除操作不会追溯删除 Apple 设备备份，历史备份的保留、删除和恢复由你的 Apple 备份设置管理；恢复旧备份后可再次执行清除。

3. 权限与用户选择

你可授予完整或有限照片访问权限，并随时在系统设置更改。有限权限只允许访问你选定的照片。照片保存权限用于你主动导出；运动信息只用于倾斜交互。应用不获取实时定位，地点来自照片已有元数据。主动分享时，选定的照片或导出内容会交给你选择的系统分享目的地，应用不会附带内部人脸特征或人物数据库。请按需选择分享对象。

4. 其他本地数据与 Apple 服务

照片索引、照片引用、星迹、偏好和使用次数保存在设备内。删除应用不会删除系统照片库原片，也不会取消订阅。照片如储存在 iCloud，系统可能按你的设置下载。内购、订阅、付款及恢复购买由 Apple App Store 处理，应用通过 StoreKit 检查购买状态；开发者不接收银行卡资料。Apple 服务与备份的数据处理受 Apple 隐私政策及你的账户设置约束。

5. 联系、网站与政策更新

主动发送支持邮件时，我们只使用你提供的邮箱地址、问题描述及自愿提供的材料来回复和排查问题，不用于广告或出售。请勿发送不必要的人脸或私人照片。可通过 atianspace8179@gmail.com 请求删除支持沟通资料。公开支持网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，访问网页时会发生正常的网页请求和托管日志处理，不会因此读取应用内照片或人物数据库。政策更新会在本页注明日期。

Privacy Policy

1. On-device photo processing

Stardrift (星云相册) is provided by Pengtian Feng. With your permission, the app accesses photos and their existing capture-date and location metadata for on-device semantic search, similar-photo tools, Star Trails and photo presentations. Photos, queries, image feature vectors and the index are not sent to developer servers or cloud model services. No account is required. The app contains no advertising tracking or third-party behavioral analytics SDK.

2. Face data

The following distinguishes data processed or stored by the app on your device from data collected or uploaded to the developer. Local processing still involves face data; the absence of an upload does not mean face data does not exist.

2.1 Types and sources

The app includes an on-device people-grouping component that processes only photos available under your Photos permission. It does not obtain face data from the camera, TrueDepth, ARKit or Face ID. During processing, Apple Vision detects face regions, facial landmarks and capture quality in memory. Landmarks align the face and are not stored separately. Locally persisted data can include 112×112 face thumbnails, numerical feature vectors for similarity matching, quality scores, local photo identifiers, group identifiers, group-average feature vectors, names entered by the user and manual exclusion flags.

2.2 Purposes and activation

The only purposes of face data are grouping the same person within your own photo library, displaying person previews, correcting groups and finding photos by a name you supplied. It is not used to establish real-world identity, authenticate users, advertise, track, sell data, match external identity databases, or train or improve machine-learning models. In the current version 1.0 (14), people grouping is off by default and the release interface has no entry point to enable face scanning. Ordinary semantic search does not require it. If an earlier internal-test installation enabled it, previously generated data may remain locally and can be cleared under Section 2.4.

2.3 Storage, disclosure and sharing

Face thumbnails, features and groups are stored in a local SwiftData database inside the app sandbox on the current device. Vision and the bundled SFace/Core ML model run on device. Face data is not transmitted to the developer, model providers, advertisers, analytics services or other third parties. The app has no face-data upload, cloud inference or cross-device person-sync function. Apple device backups may include this database according to your backup settings; this is managed by Apple device backup, not an app upload to a model service. The developer cannot access your backups.

2.4 Retention, withdrawal and deletion

Temporary face regions and landmarks are used during processing and are not stored separately. Persisted face features, thumbnails, groups and names have no automatic expiry period; they remain until you clear them or delete the app. From version 1.0 (14), open Me > Privacy controls > Clear face data and confirm. This turns off people grouping, waits for any active scan to finish, then deletes all local face records, thumbnails, group features and names while keeping original photos, the ordinary photo index and Star Trails. Clearing also works on a new installation with no face records. A deletion failure displays a retry message rather than a success message. Revoking Photos permission in system Settings stops subsequent access but does not itself erase stored data. The system Delete App action removes data for the current installation; Offload App may retain it. These actions do not retroactively delete Apple device backups. Backup retention, deletion and restoration are controlled through your Apple backup settings; after restoring an old backup, you can clear face data again.

3. Permissions and your choices

You may grant full or limited Photos access and change it in system Settings. Limited access permits only the selected photos. Add-photo access is used for exports you initiate; motion information is used only for tilt interactions. The app does not obtain live location. Locations come from existing photo metadata. Sharing sends only the photos or exported content you select to your chosen system share destination, without internal face features or the people database.

4. Other local data and Apple services

The photo index, photo references, Star Trails, preferences and usage counters are stored locally. Deleting the app neither deletes originals in the system photo library nor cancels subscriptions. The system may download iCloud photos according to your settings. Apple handles purchases, subscriptions, payments and restoration, and the app uses StoreKit to check entitlements. The developer receives no payment-card information. Apple services and backups are governed by Apple’s privacy policy and your account settings.

5. Support, website and updates

If you email support, we use your email address, issue description and voluntarily supplied materials only to reply and troubleshoot, not for advertising or sale. Avoid sending unnecessary face images or private photos. Email atianspace8179@gmail.com to request deletion of support correspondence. This public website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which handles normal webpage requests and hosting logs; visiting it does not read the app’s photos or people database. Policy updates are dated on this page.
